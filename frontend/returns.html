Returns & Replacements

Not completely satisfied? We are here to help make it right.

How to Return an Item

1. Select Item

Go to 'Your Orders' and select the item you wish to return.

2. Choose Reason

Tell us why you are returning it and choose refund or replacement.

3. Hand it Over

Pack the item. Our courier partner will pick it up from your doorstep.

General Return Policy

  • Most items can be returned within 10 days of delivery.
  • Electronics and Appliances have a 7-day replacement-only policy for defects.
  • Clothing and Shoes can be returned within 30 days, provided tags are intact.
  • Personal care, beauty products, and undergarments are non-returnable due to hygiene reasons.

Refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 3-5 business days after the returned item reaches our fulfillment center.
