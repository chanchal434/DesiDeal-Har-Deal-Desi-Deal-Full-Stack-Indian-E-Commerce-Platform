Building a Greener Future

At DesiDeal, we believe that delivering smiles shouldn't come at the cost of our planet. We are committed to achieving net-zero carbon emissions by 2040.

Sustainable Packaging

We are actively eliminating single-use plastics from our delivery network. All DesiDeal boxes are made from 100% recycled cardboard.

Electric Fleet

Our goal is to have 10,000 custom electric delivery vehicles on the road in India by the end of next year, drastically reducing our carbon footprint.

Renewable Energy

Our fulfillment centers and data hubs are transitioning to 100% renewable energy sources, utilizing massive solar arrays on facility rooftops.
