Empowering local sellers and delivering happiness to millions of homes across India. Har Deal, Desi Deal.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, DesiDeal started with a simple idea: to bridge the gap between high-quality local manufacturers and millions of Indian consumers who crave reliable, affordable products.

We noticed that massive global marketplaces were losing their personal touch. We wanted to build an ecosystem where every transaction felt secure, delivery was lightning-fast, and the deals were genuinely tailored for the desi heart.

Today, DesiDeal is more than just an e-commerce platform. It is a thriving community of over 50,000 sellers and millions of happy customers.
